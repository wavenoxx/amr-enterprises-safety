Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

At JK Safety Nets ("we," "us," or "our"), your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle the personal data you provide when interacting with our website (jksafetynets.online) or using our installation services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide you with accurate quotations and installation services. This may include:

  • Personal Details: Your full name, phone number, and email address when you fill out our contact forms or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Location Details: Your residential or commercial address strictly for the purpose of conducting free site inspections and performing the final installation.
  • Website Usage Data: Standard analytics data, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, to help us improve our website performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to facilitate our business relationship with you:

  • To schedule site visits, provide cost estimates, and coordinate installation timings.
  • To send you important service updates, invoices, and warranty documents.
  • To respond to your customer service inquiries and after-sales maintenance requests.

3. Data Protection and Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies.

Your name, address, and phone number are only shared internally with our certified installation technicians to allow them to locate your premises and perform the required services.

4. Cookies

Our website may use standard "cookies" to enhance your browsing experience. These are small text files stored on your device that help us understand how visitors use our site. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though it may affect certain website functionalities.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external platforms (such as Google Maps or WhatsApp). Once you leave our site, our Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to read the privacy statements of those external platforms.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you. You may also request that we update incorrect information or delete your details from our customer database once your warranty period expires, by contacting us directly.
