Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products and installation process.

What is the price of pigeon safety nets?

Prices depend on the total square footage area and the specific material thickness (e.g., 2.5mm vs 3mm) you choose. We offer completely free site inspections to provide you with an exact, obligation-free quote.

Are invisible grills safe for children?

Yes, absolutely. Our high-tensile SS-316 invisible grills can withstand up to 400kg of impact. They are specifically engineered to provide 100% safety for children and pets without compromising your view.

How long do safety nets last?

Our premium, UV-stabilized Garware nylon nets typically last 5 to 8 years depending on the level of direct sun exposure. They are designed to withstand harsh Indian weather conditions.

Do you provide services outside Visakhapatnam?

Yes, while our headquarters is in Visakhapatnam, we have dedicated installation teams actively serving Vijayawada, Rajahmundry, Bheemavaram, Guntur, Chennai, and tirupathi.
