About JK Safety Nets

Securing homes and commercial spaces across Andhra Pradesh with uncompromising quality and architectural integrity.

Our Heritage

Pioneers in High-Rise Safety Architecture.

Founded with a single mission—to eliminate the risks associated with high-rise living without destroying the aesthetics of modern homes. JK Safety Nets has grown from a local Visakhapatnam contractor to Andhra Pradesh's premier safety net and invisible grill enterprise.

We partner with global material manufacturers like Garware to ensure that every square inch of netting and every millimeter of steel cable we install meets strict international safety standards (ISO 9001:2015).
