Invisible Grills Installation

Modernize your home security. High-tensile stainless steel cables designed to protect children and pets on high floors without obstructing your panoramic views.

The Future of Balcony & Window Security

Traditional iron grills are bulky, prone to rusting, and make modern apartments feel like cages. Invisible Grills represent a revolution in architectural safety—providing absolute fall protection while maintaining 100% unobstructed scenic views and natural ventilation.

Engineered from marine-grade stainless steel cables, our invisible grills are perfect for high-rise residential balconies, staircases, and large glass windows. They are highly recommended for families with curious toddlers or energetic pets.

Technical Specifications

  • SS 316 Marine Grade Steel We exclusively use 316-grade stainless steel, ensuring zero rust or corrosion even in highly humid, coastal environments like Visakhapatnam and Chennai.
  • 400kg Tensile Strength Each 2mm cable is engineered to withstand up to 400kg of impact pressure. It cannot be cut by normal scissors or standard tools, offering anti-theft properties.
  • Customizable Gaps Available in 2-inch, 3-inch, and 4-inch gap distances depending on your specific requirement (e.g., 2-inch is recommended for households with cats).
  • Nylon/Teflon Coated The steel wires are coated with a smooth nylon layer. This prevents friction burns if children lean on them and makes them completely safe to touch.

Unmatched Aesthetics & Maintenance

Unlike iron that requires repainting every few years, invisible grills are zero-maintenance. A simple wipe with a damp cloth keeps them shining. They can be installed vertically or horizontally, seamlessly integrating into your building's architecture without violating society guidelines.

Expert Installation & Warranty

Our invisible grills are anchored using heavy-duty Hilti fasteners and aluminum tracks with internal stiffeners to maintain high tension. Backed by a 5-Year Warranty against rusting and structural failure.
